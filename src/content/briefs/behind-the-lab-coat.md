## What we did

We showed nearly 1,500 people the Twitter profiles of scientists. The profiles differed in how much they posted about their personal lives versus their professional work. We then measured how people perceived the scientists, how they engaged with the tweets, and how they felt about science.

## What we found

- Personal posts made scientists seem more likable but less competent than professional posts did.
- Scientists’ presence on social media itself benefited how people perceived and interacted with them.

## Why it matters

There is a real tension in how scientists present themselves online. The lesson is not simply to post less about your life. It is to think about how your whole online presence lines up with how you want to be seen.
