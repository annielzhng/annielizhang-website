## What we did

Past science controversies point to three drivers of polarization: cues from political elites, trust in public health institutions, and differences in where people get information. We tested each one using the Axios/Ipsos Coronavirus Index, which surveyed about 1,000 Americans nearly every week from March 2020 to September 2021.

## What we found

- Trust in public health institutions explained the most about partisan reactions to COVID-19.
- Over time, Republicans grew less trusting of these institutions and Democrats grew more trusting, a split that sharpened after the 2020 election.

## Why it matters

When elite polarization and trust in science become entangled, political resistance to science-based policy can have lasting effects. Breaking that cycle matters for communicating science in future crises.
